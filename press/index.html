About the game

Worlds have collided. The old gods have fallen, a new order of storm gods has seized the heavens, and their dragon rains destruction from the sky. Cloudburst is a real-time match-3: steer each falling gem into place, line up three of a colour, set off chain reactions, and swap gems inside the stack to save yourself before the storm buries you.

Clare, the last goddess loyal to the old ways, lends you the powers of the old gods. Each of the eight new gods can only be destroyed by a relic of the old ones, which the player must find and assemble piece by piece.

Key features

  • 80 levels across 8 worlds, from Marble Meadow to the Sky Kingdom
  • 8 boss fights: Grimmoss, the Ice Queen, Magmaw, Twistor, the Void Eye, Drizzle, Thunderhead and King Nimbus
  • Clare's Wrath powers: Thor's hammer, the Sunbeam of Ra, Freeze, Spiders, Lightning, Meteor shower, the Black hole and a three-headed Dragon
  • Relics of the old gods: the Sky Sword, the Head of Medusa, the Trident of the Deep, Zeus's thunderbolt and more
  • Daily speed challenge with a Google Play Games world leaderboard
  • Daily quests, a daily gift, quakes, lightning bolts and boosters
  • Shaped gems and a colour-blind mode
