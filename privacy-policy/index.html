Privacy Policy for Cloudburst

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Who we are

Cloudburst: Wrath of the Gods (“Cloudburst”) is a game made by Carl Morton.

Your game progress

Your levels, stars, dabloons, lightning bolts and settings are saved only on your device. There are no Cloudburst accounts, and this information is not sent to us.

Leaderboard (Google Play Games)

If you play the Daily Speed Challenge, the game can post your survival time to the Google Play Games leaderboard. To do this you sign in with your Google Play Games profile, and your Play Games name, avatar and score are shown to other players on the leaderboard. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage your Play Games profile and visibility in the Google Play Games app.

Advertising

Cloudburst shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address and general app usage, as described in the Google Privacy Policy. Players in the UK and EU are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change this choice at any time in the game’s Settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone’s Settings → Google → Ads.

Purchases

Any in-game purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

Permissions

The game uses vibration for haptic feedback. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

What we don’t do

We don’t collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos or files, and we don’t sell any information.

Children

Cloudburst is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, email mortoncarl2016@gmail.com.
